- Admission Process
- Freshman
- Transfer Student
Content:
Applicants are considered a “first-time freshman” if the students’ first enrollment at a college or university, excluding summer, will occur at UT Dallas within two years of graduating from high school. Applicants are still considered a “first-time freshman” if they earn college credit before high school graduation (i.e., dual enrollment courses).
Applicants are encouraged to apply to UT Dallas through ApplyTexas or Common Application during their senior year of high school in order to be considered for admission and scholarship opportunities.
Applicants are considered a “transfer student” if they have previously completed college-level coursework at an accredited institution (excluding the summer following high school). Applicants can apply to UT Dallas through ApplyTexas or Common Application to start during the fall, spring and summer semesters.

Yes. We accept both the GMAT and the GRE equally. Applicants are encouraged to submit the standardized test score that best reflects their quantitative and analytical strengths, as the admissions committee holds no preference between the two exams.
We do not enforce a strict minimum GMAT / GRE score because we evaluate every candidate holistically. However, to help you gauge competitiveness, the average GMAT score for the Full-Time MBA Class of 2023 was 689, with 80% of accepted candidates scoring between 650 and 700. For the Professional MBA program, scores typically range from 600 to 700, with the Professional Evening Cohort averaging a 648. For all MBA programs, average GRE scores range from 310-320.
While the GMAT or GRE are required for admission, all MBA candidates are automatically considered for a test waiver if all required application components have been submitted. GMAT/GRE test waivers will be evaluated based on Academic Readiness, Quantitative and Analytic Strength, Leadership and Professional Maturity, and Supplemental Evidence.
GMAT/GRE Test requirement is automatically waived if applicants have any of the following criteria: PhD, PharmD, or DBA or a U.S. based MD, JD or an MCAT or LSAT score report. Please note that if an applicant qualifies and is granted a GMAT/GRE test waiver, the committee will review the applicant’s file without a test score, but it does not guarantee admission into the MBA program. If the applicant does not qualify and the test waiver is not granted, the applicant must submit a GMAT/GRE test score in order to continue with the admission process.
